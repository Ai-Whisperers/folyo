# Product Vision & Core Rules

## Fundamental Philosophy
**This is NOT just a CV builder.**
The goal is to generate **Portfolio Webpages** for professionals of ALL industries.

## Target Audience
- **Traditional**: Administration, Accounting, Doctors.
- **Creative**: Developers, Audiovisual Majors, Designers.

## Core Differentiators
1.  **Web-First Experience**: We are not optimizing for A4 printable PDFs. We are creating beautiful, interactive websites that showcase a person's work.
2.  **Rich Media Support**: specifically for audiovisual professionals, the platform MUST support:
    - Video embedding/playback.
    - Photo galleries/carousels.
    - Script/Screenplay display options.
3.  **Universal Appeal**: Must afford the structure needed for a doctor (publications, certifications) while providing the flexibility needed for a video editor (showreels, visual portfolios).

## Source of Truth Rules
1.  **Output Format**: The primary artifact is a deployed website, not a downloadable file (though PDF export can be a secondary feature).
2.  **Design Priority**: Visuals, interactivity, and mobile responsiveness take precedence over "standard resume formatting".
3.  **Assets**: Users must be able to upload or link rich media (Youtube, Vimeo, Images) easily.
