# Competitor Analysis & Feature Strategy

## Competitor Breakdown

### 1. Resume.io
-   **Core Promise**: "Land your next job" via optimized resume documents.
-   **Key Features**:
    -   AI Writer & Spell checker.
    -   Job Tracker (wishlist, applied, interviewed).
    -   Export: PDF/Word.
-   **Weakness**: Purely focused on the *document*. No mention of hosting content, portfolios, or rich media. It's an artifact generator, not a presence builder.

### 2. OnlineCV.es
-   **Core Promise**: Speed ("Perfect CV in 5 minutes").
-   **Key Features**:
    -   ATS-optimized templates.
    -   Multilingual editor.
    -   "AI" text generation.
-   **Weakness**: Extremely transactional. "Download and go." No concept of a persistent online profile.

### 3. CVwizard
-   **Core Promise**: Simple 3-step process (Fill -> Template -> Download).
-   **Key Features**:
    -   Cover letter builder.
    -   Job application organizer.
-   **Weakness**: Generic. The output is indistinguishable from 100 other tools.

## The Gap: "The Professional Portfolio Webpage"
None of these tools address the needs of:
-   **Audiovisual Professionals**: Who need to show, not tell. A PDF cannot play a showreel.
-   **Modern Professionals**: Who share LinkedIn or Website links, not physical papers.

## What We Will Copy (The Prerequisites)
-   **Ease of Use**: The "Flow" (Step 1: Contact, Step 2: Experience) is standard for a reason. We will keep this linear data entry.
-   **Templates**: We need attractive starting points, but ours will be *responsive web templates*, not *A4 page layouts*.
-   **AI Helpers**: Auto-generating summaries is a must-have commodity feature.

## What We Will Innovate (The Differentiators)
-   **"My Showreel" Section**: Dedicated slot for Vimeo/Youtube embeds.
-   **Gallery Modules**: Grid/Carousel for photographers and designers.
-   **Persistent URL**: `profile.io/username` instead of `download.pdf`.
-   **Live Analytics**: "Who viewed my portfolio" (similar to Resume.io but for the *site*).
